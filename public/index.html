HISTORY OF COMPUTING

Why is it called "The Moth in the Machine"?

HARVARD COMPUTATION LAB • 1947

"1545 Relay #70 Panel F (moth) in relay."

🦋 [Moth Specimen]

"First actual case of bug being found."

On September 9, 1947, computer scientist Grace Hopper and her team at Harvard University were working on the electromechanical Mark II computer when a relay stopped working.

When technicians investigated, they found a real moth stuck between the electrical contact points of Relay 70 in Panel F!

They carefully removed the moth with tweezers, taped it to the logbook, and wrote: "First actual case of bug being found."

While the word "bug" had been used in engineering, Grace Hopper's story made the terms "bug" and "debugging" a universal foundation of modern software engineering.
